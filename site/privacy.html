Privacy

Last updated 3 November 2026

Short version: this website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other services. The AssayPlot app has no telemetry and keeps your data on your computer. The details follow, as the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) asks.

Who is responsible

Faris Hrvat, who develops AssayPlot. Write to AssayPlot@pm.me about anything on this page.

This website

The site is static pages hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc., with a copy on GitLab Pages by GitLab, Inc. When you open a page, your browser sends the host your IP address and the usual request details: the page asked for, the time, your browser and operating system, and the page you came from. The host needs this to deliver the page and keeps it in its server logs to protect the service against abuse. I don't see these logs and get no visitor statistics.

The legal basis is legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in running a reachable, secure website. GitHub and GitLab are based in the United States. They transfer data under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses, as their own privacy statements describe: GitHub, GitLab.

There are no cookies, so there is no cookie banner. The pages load no fonts, scripts, images or embeds from anywhere else. Links to GitLab or GitHub take you to those sites, where their own privacy terms apply.

Email

If you write to me, I use your address and message only to answer you, and delete them when the matter is closed unless we keep talking. The mailbox is hosted by Proton AG in Switzerland, which the EU recognises as giving adequate protection.

The AssayPlot app

  • Your data, projects and figures stay on your computer. Nothing is uploaded.
  • There is no account, no telemetry and no crash reporting.
  • To find new versions, the app asks GitHub for the latest release when it starts, or GitLab if GitHub can't be reached. They see your IP address, as with any web request. Turn this off under Settings → Check for updates.
  • The Notion export only runs if you connect it yourself. Your Notion token is stored on your computer, and what you export goes straight from your computer to Notion. Notion's own privacy terms apply there.

Issues and contributions

If you open an issue or a merge request on GitLab or GitHub, what you post is public and handled under that platform's terms.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your personal data, and for it to be corrected, deleted or restricted. You can also object to its use and ask for a copy you can take elsewhere (Art. 15–21 GDPR). Since this site and the app collect nothing themselves, in practice this concerns emails you sent me. For server logs, contact GitHub or GitLab. You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Italy the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, or the authority of the EU country where you live or work.

Changes

If what the site or the app does with data changes, this page changes first. The date at the top shows the last update.
